## What the transcripts are

The files under `data/transcripts/` are the **automatic YouTube captions** of the public
videos of the "Business Central Launch Event - 2026 release wave 2", published by
Microsoft on the Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central YouTube channel on 2026-10-01.

- They are machine generated. Expect transcription errors, missing punctuation, wrong
  product names and misspelled presenter names.
- Nobody at Microsoft reviewed them. Nothing here is an official statement about the
  product, its roadmap or its availability.
- Every derived page (summaries, feature pages, reports, the site) links back to the
  exact second in the original video. The video is the source, this repository is the
  index.

## Who owns what

- The videos and their captions are Microsoft's content. They are reproduced here only to
  make the public launch event searchable and navigable, with attribution and deep links
  on every page.
- The code (pipeline, site, scripts, workflows) is MIT licensed, see `LICENSE`.
- The summaries and feature pages were generated by a language model from the captions
  and are provided as-is.

## Private versus public

While this repository is private, the full cleaned transcripts live in
`data/transcripts/full/` and the raw captions in `data/transcripts/raw/`.

Before it goes public, `scripts/strip-for-public.sh` removes the full transcripts, the
raw captions, the LLM cache (which contains transcript text) and the full-text search
passages. What remains are summaries, feature pages and short quotes of at most 30
words, each with a link to the moment in the video where it is said.

If you are the rights holder and want something removed, open an issue on the repository
or contact waldo at waldo.be.
