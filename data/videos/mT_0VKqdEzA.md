# What's new in Expense Agent: Calculate WHT for Expenses

> This video explains how withholding tax, which already existed for vendors, now also works for employees in Expense Agent. The presenter first covers what withholding tax is and why it matters, then lists the setup options: posting groups, gross or net calculation, simple or compound calculation, and thresholds. A demo creates a mileage expense of about $2,127 above a $1,000 threshold and submits it. After posting, a withholding tax entry of 20% appears (amount 425.40). The presenter then walks through the withholding posting setup, the employee card and the expense category. A separate finance session is mentioned for use outside expense management.
