# What's new in E-Documents: Overview

> This video introduces EDI support in Business Central 2026 release wave 2, built on top of the existing E-Documents framework. It explains the buyer and seller flow: purchase order, automatic sales order, order response and remittance advice. Ken covers the setup requirements, then Milita demos the full flow manually with the PEPPOL format across two companies. The last part shows that the same e-document and message architecture is used for three new French regulatory formats, with lifecycle events stored as messages linked to the invoice e-document.
