# What's new in Finance: Overview

> This video gives an overview of the finance changes in the 2026 wave 2 release. It covers improvements to excise taxes and self-billing that were first delivered in an earlier wave, and a short look at cross environment master data management. It then covers withholding tax for employees, which also works outside the expense agent, and a round-up of localization updates for Spain, France, Australia, Great Britain, Germany, Switzerland, Austria and Iceland. Several excise and localization items are marked as coming in November or December rather than in the initial release.
