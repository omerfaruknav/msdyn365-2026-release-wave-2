# What's new in SCM: Subcontracting

> The video introduces the Subcontracting app for Business Central, which was quietly released in minor update three and is now pre-installed on new environments. Existing environments can install it from Marketplace, AppSource or from the legacy Subcontracting worksheet. The demo follows one order through the flow: the component supply method on BOM lines, production order components and routing, the subcontracting purchase order, comments and attachments, and transfer orders with WIP items and a WIP ledger entry. It then covers item charge assignment to receipts, closest-match subcontractor pricing with a minimum amount, and receiving through warehouse receipts and inventory put-away with serial numbers. Almost no preview or GA labels are given, apart from a remark that one warehouse scenario already existed in minor release 28.3.
