# What's new in SCM: Migrate Italian Subcontracting

> This session is for Italian customers and partners. It explains how to move from the old Italian subcontracting functionality to the new global Subcontracting app, which replaces it. The presenter walks through a Contoso Italy company: the legacy toggle in Manufacturing Setup, the pre-check, cleaning up open transfer and purchase documents with work in progress items, installing the apps, and running the migration. He then shows what changes afterwards (fields, pages, views, prices) and lists the requirements and what data gets migrated. Migration can now run in production, based on customer and partner feedback.
