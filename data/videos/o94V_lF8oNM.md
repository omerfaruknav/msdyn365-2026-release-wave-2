# What's new in Expense Agent: Travel and Expense Policy Automation

> This video demos a new policy compliance function in the Expense Agent for Business Central. An admin turns on AI evaluation of free-text expense policies and optionally lets submitters run a check before submitting. The expense agent web app shows compliant, flagged and policies-pending statuses with the reason for each flag. The speaker also covers submitting with pending policies, approver overrides, and two items that are coming later: receipt itemization and a mandatory override reason.
