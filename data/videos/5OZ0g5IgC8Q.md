# What's new: Shopify Tax Matching (preview)

> The video shows an AI-based Tax Matching capability in the Shopify connector for US customers, where sales tax depends on state, city and other levels. It explains how the connector handles tax today, then demos importing orders where the AI assigns or creates tax areas, tax jurisdictions and tax details in Business Central. The presenter walks through the review and approve window, the confidence indicators and the settings fact box on the Shopify shop card. A later demo shows an order stopped because the Business Central tax rate differs from the Shopify rate, and how to approve with either rate. The capability is US only and ships as a small extension that is not on AppSource.
