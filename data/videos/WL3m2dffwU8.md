# Introducing: New Microsoft Copilot Chat in Business Central

> This short video introduces the new Microsoft Copilot chat experience in Business Central, which replaces the old Copilot UI. It is the same Copilot experience used across other Microsoft products. It answers questions using Business Central data, respecting the user's permissions and context. With the additional Microsoft Copilot license it can also use Work IQ data such as calendar, email, Teams and files. A short demo asks about Copilot features, then about the most urgent sales orders, opens the data side by side and follows up with a question about an Adatum fulfillment backlog.
