# What's new: Manage PTEs in the Admin Center

> This video covers three app management changes in Business Central 2026 release wave 2. The main one is full lifecycle management of per-tenant extensions (PTEs) in the admin center. Upload, install and update are added, with deployment schedules (immediate, next minor update, next major update), cancelable future schedules and an operations page that audits everything. The speakers then cover the deprecation of the old PTE management experience and API, simpler permissions for S2S apps, and early installation of hotfixes for Microsoft apps. The last item is a small change that keeps dev extensions installed on sandbox environments during lifecycle operations.
