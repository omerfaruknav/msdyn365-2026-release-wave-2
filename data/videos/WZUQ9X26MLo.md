# What's new in Sustainability: EUDR Certificate Capture

> The video explains how Business Central Sustainability now helps capture EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) data for items that are sold into the EU market. Items are flagged as EUDR relevant on the item card, and certificate details are stored per lot on the Lot No. Information card, so item tracking is required. Purchase lines get an EUDR flag, and sales invoice layouts can print the lot, certification schema and certification number. The speaker says there is no integration with Traces yet, so DDS numbers and geolocation must be entered by hand. Viewers are asked to send feedback through Viva Engage.
