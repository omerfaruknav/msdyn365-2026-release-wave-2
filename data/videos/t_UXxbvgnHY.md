# What's new in Sustainability

> This video covers the sustainability changes in 2026 release wave two. Formulas now work in purchase orders and purchase invoices, and value chain emissions reach more journals and service management. Scope 3 emissions can be tracked per item tracking level, and the ESG report can print per lot. Sustainability ledger entries get a reverse button, and Collect Amount from GL now suggests only the remaining amount. An end-to-end demo with coffee machines walks through purchase, sale, item journals, reclassification, a service order, reversal, fixed assets and the collect-from-GL change.
