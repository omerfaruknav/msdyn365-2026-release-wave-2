# What they didn't say - 2026 release wave 2

Microsoft no longer publishes per-feature release plans. The baseline here is the official documentation: 81 documented features (docs-preview-features, docs-whatsnew-overview, ai-at-work-roadmap, fetched 2026-10-02). Against that: 59 documented features were shown or discussed in the videos, 22 were not, 178 things from the videos have no documented counterpart, and 46 features have a status conflict between docs and video (6 where the presenters stated the status, the rest where they said nothing and GA is implied by launch event convention while the docs say preview).

The stage time went to subcontracting and BC-Bench, and almost none of it maps to the docs. Supply-chain got a long run of short slots on the Subcontracting app, WIP items and routing details. Developer-tools spent several slots on benchmarking AI code review. Most of the undocumented items look like pieces of a bigger feature, so they were shown as part of a story, not as their own line item.

The reverse gap is quieter. Documented items that never appeared are mostly copilot-and-agents polish, AL tooling tweaks and small admin conveniences like recently searched in Tell Me. The status conflicts follow one pattern. Under our rule, nothing said means GA, and the docs call a large block of expense, withholding tax and EDI items preview. Presenters apparently treat the word preview as optional. Developers should not.
