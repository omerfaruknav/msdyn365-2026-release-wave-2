# What they didn't say - 2026 release wave 2

Microsoft no longer publishes per-feature release plans. The baseline here is the official documentation: 81 documented features (docs-preview-features, docs-whatsnew-overview, ai-at-work-roadmap, fetched 2026-10-02). Against that: 59 documented features were shown or discussed in the videos, 22 were not, 174 things from the videos have no documented counterpart, and 8 features have a status conflict between docs and video.

The stage time went to subcontracting and BC-Bench, and much of it never made it into the docs. Most of the undocumented items carry no stated status, so either the demos ran ahead of the paperwork or the paperwork is late. Plenty of them are the supporting cast: pre-checks, fact boxes, a toggle, a setup step. Useful, just not headline material.

The reverse gap is quieter. Documented but not shown are the agent UI polish, AL tooling conveniences and small web client touches like recently used in lookups. Nobody films a lookup. Then there are the status conflicts: the videos said announced for themes and Hide if expressions where the docs say GA, and called Copilot chat in Business Central GA where the docs say preview. Check the docs before you promise anything to a customer.
